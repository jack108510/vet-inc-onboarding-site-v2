What is FeeWatch

Retrospective analysis, not forecasting.

FeeWatch is a pricing analysis service from Vet INC. It studies your clinic's historical records — specifically, how fee schedule changes you've made in the past correlated with changes in visit volume, service utilization, and billing outcomes over the same period. The findings are retrospective: we're looking at patterns that already exist in your data, not making predictions or prescribing prices.

The goal is straightforward: when you're deciding whether to adjust a service fee, it helps to understand what happened at your clinic the last time you did. FeeWatch surfaces those patterns so that context is available when you need it.

The findings

What the analysis can surface.

The specific patterns depend on what your data contains. Here's the kind of picture a retrospective analysis can draw.

Visit volume trends

How overall visit counts moved in the periods surrounding historical fee change events at your clinic.

Sales & service use

Whether specific service codes saw changes in use frequency in the periods following fee adjustments.

Revenue trajectory

How revenue moved across the time periods your data covers, relative to when pricing events occurred.

Differential signals

Which areas of your fee schedule appear more or less correlated with outcome changes, based on the historical record.

Quiet periods

Times where fee changes occurred without clear correlation — which is itself useful context when evaluating past decisions.

A plain-language summary

Everything translated out of data-speak. We present findings in a format that's useful for a practice owner or manager, not a data analyst.

A note on what patterns can and can't tell you. The findings from FeeWatch reflect correlations between events in your historical data. They do not establish that your fee changes caused any particular outcome — other factors always play a role. The analysis is designed to inform your thinking, not to replace your judgement, and it carries no guarantee about what future pricing changes will do.
